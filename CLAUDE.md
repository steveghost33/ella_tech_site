# Working rules for this site (from the owner)

- Lead with businesses and small businesses. The site is a marketing tool for them first.
- Never lead with nonprofits. Nonprofit work should be easy to find, but secondary.
- Do not position the firm as Detroit only. Detroit based is fine; the audience is not limited to Detroit.
- No full redesigns. Make small, targeted edits to the existing site and keep its structure, sections and look.
- Show proposed copy and changes before editing, and confirm scope before committing or pushing.
- Writing: plain and service forward. No em dashes or double hyphens, no emojis, no buzzwords, no urgency tactics.
- Never invent clients, testimonials, results, numbers or credentials.
- Do not present the Detroit Public Schools Community District website as Ella Tech work.
